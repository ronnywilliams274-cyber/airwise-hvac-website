    FAQ

    Questions? Answered.

    How much does a new system cost?

    It depends on your home's size, ductwork and the efficiency you want. That's why the on-site assessment is free and you get good / better / best options in writing before any work starts.

    How long does an installation take?

    Most residential change-outs take a single day. Bigger retrofits or ductwork changes can take 2–3 days. We'll give you a firm schedule up front.

    Why does an HVAC company do paint jobs?

    Because our customers asked. We already work on your home's exterior, equipment and ducts. Painting walls, trim, equipment and protective coatings with the same trusted crew means one schedule and one invoice.

    Do you work with builders and contractors?

    Yes. We handle load calcs, duct design, rough-in and final for new construction, and we build our schedule around yours.

    How often should my system be serviced?

    Twice a year is the industry rule of thumb: cooling in spring and heating in fall. Regular tune-ups catch failing parts early and keep efficiency up.
